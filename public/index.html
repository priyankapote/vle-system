From order to refill

01

Order placed

A customer requests a cylinder type, size and quantity. The admin is notified and checks stock.

02

Assigned & delivered

Admin assigns an available delivery person. The cylinder moves to In Transit, then In Use on arrival.

03

Collected empty

The customer marks it empty and ready for pickup. A delivery person collects it for refilling.

04

Back in stock

Once refilled and returned to storage, the cylinder is marked In Stock and ready to go out again.
